PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy Matters

At Menuchah Haven, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your personal data.

INTRODUCTION

Our Commitment to Privacy

Menuchah Haven values your trust. Whether you visit our website, submit a prayer request, subscribe to updates, or contact us, we are committed to handling your information responsibly and with respect. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, protect, and manage your information.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Information You Choose to Share

Personal Information

Information such as your name, email address, or any details you provide through contact forms or prayer requests.

Website Usage

Basic technical information such as browser type, device information, and pages visited to help improve the website.

Cookies

Small files that help remember preferences and improve your browsing experience.

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

Why We Collect Information

  • To respond to enquiries and prayer requests.
  • To provide ministry updates if you subscribe.
  • To improve the website and user experience.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of our services.
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

DATA PROTECTION

Keeping Your Information Safe

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. While no internet transmission is completely secure, we continually seek to maintain appropriate safeguards for the information entrusted to us.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

External Services

Menuchah Haven may use trusted third-party services for website hosting, analytics, email communication, media streaming, and other essential functions. These providers have their own privacy practices, and we encourage you to review their policies where applicable.

YOUR RIGHTS

Your Choices

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, ask for corrections, request deletion of your information, or withdraw your consent where applicable. We will make reasonable efforts to honor such requests where legally permitted.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Protecting Young Visitors

Menuchah Haven does not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where required by law. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our website, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

POLICY UPDATES

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our ministry, website features, or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

QUESTIONS?

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us through the Contact page. We will do our best to respond promptly and transparently.
