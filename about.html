About Menuchah Haven

A place where weary hearts encounter the presence of God, discover His truth, and find lasting rest in Christ.

OUR STORY

Why Menuchah Haven Exists

Menuchah Haven was born from a longing to create a quiet place where people can encounter the presence of God in the midst of life's noise and distractions. Menuchah Haven exists because the world has become increasingly noisy, hurried, and spiritually distracted. Many people long for peace but struggle to find a quiet place where they can truly encounter God.

We believe true rest is found in Christ alone. Through biblical teaching, worship, prayer, reflections, and practical resources, we desire to help people draw closer to Him every day.

Our prayer is that every visitor leaves encouraged, strengthened, and transformed by God's Word.

OUR JOURNEY

How Menuchah Haven Continues to Grow

The Calling

God placed a burden to create a place where people could encounter His presence, hear His Word, and find true rest.

The Beginning

Menuchah Haven was established as a Christ-centered ministry dedicated to biblical teaching, worship, prayer, and spiritual encouragement.

The Mission Today

Through media, resources, and community, Menuchah Haven seeks to help people grow in faith and experience the peace found in Jesus Christ.

The Vision Ahead

To reach people across nations with faithful biblical teaching and create a lasting legacy of spiritual renewal for generations to come.

OUR VISION

Our Vision

To become a trusted place of spiritual renewal where people from every nation encounter God's presence, grow in His Word, and experience the peace found only in Jesus Christ.

OUR FOUNDATION

What We Believe

Menuchah Haven is firmly rooted in the authority of the Holy Scriptures. We believe that the Bible is God's inspired and trustworthy Word, revealing His character, His will, and His plan of salvation through Jesus Christ.

We believe that salvation is found by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone, who died for our sins, rose again, and reigns forever as Lord and Saviour.

We believe the Holy Spirit empowers believers to grow in holiness, live according to God's Word, and faithfully share the Gospel with the world.

FOUNDER

Meet the Founder

Menuchah Haven was founded by Lotanna Edwin Okeke with a desire to create a place where people can encounter the presence of God through Scripture, worship, prayer, and Christ-centred teaching.

His vision is to help people slow down, hear God's voice, deepen their relationship with Jesus Christ, and live out their faith with confidence, hope, and purpose.
